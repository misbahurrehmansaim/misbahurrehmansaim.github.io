I find what's wrong, what's missing and what competitors are doing, then help fix it.

Pick a stage to see how I work through a business. The example is illustrative, not client data.

    1. I start with the business: what it sells, who buys it, how people find it today and where its leads and sales come from.

    2. I go through the website and its marketing: SEO, content, speed, mobile experience, ads and the path to conversion.

    3. Issues come back as a ranked list: what's broken, slow, missing or confusing, and what it's likely costing.

    4. I look at who is winning the same searches and customers, and what they do that this business doesn't.

    5. The gaps between what competitors cover and what the business offers become the opportunities.

    6. I choose the approach that fits the goal, whether that's SEO, paid ads, content, website changes or a mix, and put it in priority order.

    7. I help carry it out: on-page fixes, new content, campaigns and landing pages.

    8. I track traffic, rankings, leads and conversions, and report what moved and what to do next.

    Baby Store Canada

    E-commerce, baby products. Canada.

    SEO Specialist, May 2025 to Aug 2026. Freelance, remote.

    Organic traffic, +150%

    Bars show each reported change relative to the starting point.

    Problem
    An online baby-products store with hundreds of product pages that needed to be found for its key categories and to turn more of that traffic into orders.
    Research
    Keyword research across product categories and parenting topics, plus a review of on-page, structured data, page speed and mobile usability gaps.
    Strategy
    A full SEO overhaul: optimize category and product pages, add structured data, build content around parenting and baby care topics, and improve speed and mobile usability.
    Implementation
    On-page optimization across hundreds of product pages, product schema markup, a content plan around parenting and baby care, and site speed and mobile fixes.
    Result
    150% increase in organic traffic, top-3 rankings for key product categories, and a 40% improvement in conversion rate.

    • Page speed
